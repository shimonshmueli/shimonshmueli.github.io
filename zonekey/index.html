Features

Organize your apps with tags, switch between running apps with keyboard shortcuts, and enjoy a clean, distraction-free experience. And with your input I am planing to make it even better.

  • Instant Search

    Fuzzy and keyword matching finds apps even with partial or misspelled queries.

  • Smart Tags

    Create any number of tags to organize your apps — Work, Creative, Social, whatever fits.

  • App Switcher

    View and cycle through running apps with a keyboard shortcut. Exclude any you don't want in the rotation.

  • Keyboard-first

    Arrow keys to navigate, Enter to launch, Escape to dismiss. Hands stay on the keyboard.

  • Drag & Drop

    Drag apps to the Dock, to tags, or between views.

  • Tag Colors

    Color-code tags for quick visual organization.

  • Export & Import

    Take your settings with you — back them up or move between machines.
